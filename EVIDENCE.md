# Evidence and maturity

This guide distinguishes observed behavior, implemented components and intended product behavior. Evidence comes from retained internal records. The underlying code, prompts, private materials and raw run evidence are not published here; this is not a self-contained reproduction package.

The October 26 documentary review draws on retained September and October artifacts, including the October 14 cross-product readiness assessment, technical copies retained on October 20, and Infinite Manuscript's October 22 alpha and later visual records. It is not a fresh deployment audit. No new experiments were run for this presentation; each milestone remains attached to its own project and version.

Sheila coordinates presentation and release review; Arden prepared the source-grounded showcase candidate and its editorial successor. Team review is not external replication. The editorial pass changes presentation only, without new source inspection or application tests.

## Roleplay

**Historical live demonstration, October 11, 2026.** The October 14 readiness review records two real model responses in an isolated, operator-assisted run. One character retained an attributed statement; another had no corresponding account. Subsequent dialogue reflected that distinction.

This supports a connected, narrow demonstration of character-specific continuity. The original conversation was still provided. It does not establish memory-only reconstruction, long-session performance, general automatic consequence interpretation or model-level secrecy when multiple perspectives share a generation context.

**Campaign construction, reviewed October 14.** The review records implemented draft discussion, revision, compiled review, approval and launch wiring, with offline acceptance. It also identifies unfinished general consequence selection and an unproved ordinary-user restart-and-return path. Offline acceptance is not acceptance of a complete live campaign workflow.

## Story

**Design and prototype evidence, September 2026.** Retained story-continuity documents distinguish established events, character beliefs, relationships and unresolved threads. The related Solo Continuity Alpha prototype has file-backed state and authored checks for accepted versus rejected alternatives, divergent branches, and invalid history links.

For this presentation, prototype source and checks were inspected without execution. Its README reports successful proofs; that remains a historical internal report, not a fresh result. Neither those checks nor the story designs establish a finished writing interface, whole-book continuity performance, or better prose.

**Related application, October 22, 2026.** [Infinite Manuscript](https://github.com/sheilastudios/infinite-manuscript/blob/main/EVIDENCE.md) has a separately documented director-led local alpha and later continuity-aware visual workflows. Retained evidence includes actual synthetic-story continuations, an author revision, generated scene images, anticipatory withholding and fresh selection, image/portrait reuse, and browser/recovery work. Later records also preserve a physical-state narration inconsistency and pending browser regression; the expanded goal remains open. Selected RP/Companion component reuse is documented, separately from the earlier Atelier module proposal. None of these relationships transfers Infinite Manuscript's results to RP Host or establishes a single integrated three-pillar release.

## Companion

**Builder and earlier interaction, September-October 2026.** Builder documentation records conversational creation, deterministic compilation, human-readable review, package verification and bounded live evaluations. The October 14 readiness assessment records earlier live client operation and a later four-call recall exercise with two recall queries. Prompted title recall was observed; that exercise did not establish retention of newly generated nonempty final memories.

**Later continuity components.** An isolated October 10 transition-host contract and retained implementation describe source-bound updates, historical meaning retention and recovery. Its examples are authored mock responses. The contract explicitly admits that a structurally valid account can still overstate meaning. Mechanical checks are not semantic or behavioral validation.

**Operator view and portability, October 12.** Review records accept a saved/mock viewer and a narrow backup/restore correction. Viewer evidence concerns saved material, not new companion interaction. The portability demonstration was isolated and platform-specific; it does not establish ordinary customer migration or Windows-backed publication.

## What remains open

- A selected, reproducible integrated release with end-to-end user workflows.
- Sustained return and recall under ordinary use, beyond the bounded demonstrations.
- Whole-book continuity and literary efficacy beyond Infinite Manuscript's bounded local Story alpha.
- Broad affect, identity-growth and portability claims.
- Public hosting readiness, multiplayer operation and external replication.

These are evidence gaps in the reviewed set, not claims that later work cannot exist. Historical blockers should be refreshed before describing current operational status.

## Demonstration recommendation

An accessible next presentation would use three short, wholly synthetic examples: a player choice with unequal witness knowledge; an author's rejected alternate that does not contaminate the selected story; and a companion returning to an unfinished matter without confusing a played role with ongoing identity.

This is a proposed demonstration, not an observed combined result. A narrative illustration must be labeled as such. A runtime demonstration would require separately authorized execution, a selected version, retained outcomes and disclosure review. No such new run was performed for this showcase.
