# RP Host

## Roleplay. Story. Companion.

**RP Host is Sheila Studios' privately developed project family for interactive worlds, sustained fiction, and ongoing AI companionship.** Its central question is what should carry forward when a conversation or scene ends: not only the words, but the consequences, relationships, unfinished matters, and differences in what each participant knows.

These are three distinct experiences. An author may revise a story. A player should retain control of their own character. A companion's ongoing identity should not be rewritten as though it were an unwanted draft.

This is a **documentation showcase, not a software release**. The work includes implemented components, historical internal demonstrations, and designs awaiting further integration. Source code and private implementation are not included. [Evidence and maturity](EVIDENCE.md) explains the boundaries.

## Roleplay: choices that have a history

The roleplay work joins campaign creation with the problem that comes after a good first scene: making later interaction respect what actually happened.

The project includes campaign-authoring and launch work, character-specific knowledge, unresolved matters, and the distinction between an offered continuation and an accepted consequence. A claim made in dialogue should not automatically become a fact about the world. Nor should hearing it inform every other character.

An internal October 2026 demonstration exercised that distinction over two turns: a statement was retained for the character who heard it, while a second character had no corresponding account. The first recalled it; the second said they had not heard it. This was an operator-assisted example with the original dialogue still available, not proof of unlimited memory or perfect knowledge isolation.

**Maturity:** implemented campaign-authoring components and a bounded live continuity demonstration. General consequence handling for custom campaigns and a complete leave-and-return user journey remained unfinished in the reviewed October 14 assessment. Multiplayer is a design horizon, not a demonstrated released feature.

## Story: continuity in service of the work

Story mode addresses authored fiction rather than player-directed interaction. Its design keeps character relationships, scene and arc history, unresolved tensions, and viewpoint distinctions available to later writing without treating every abandoned alternative as part of the story.

The important distinctions are literary as well as technical. A character can be wrong without the story contradicting itself. Two people can remember the same encounter differently. An unresolved relationship can remain unresolved rather than being summarized into agreement. A revised passage should not quietly leave its discarded consequences behind.

The retained RP Host work includes story-oriented continuity designs, structured authoring workflows, and a related file-backed prototype with recovery and branch-integrity checks. A distinct related application, **[Infinite Manuscript](https://github.com/sheilastudios/infinite-manuscript)**, is a visual, continuity-aware storytelling studio: imagery, motifs and atmosphere develop alongside authored prose, with character readings and reusable scene artwork.

Infinite Manuscript's October 22 director-led alpha checkpoint includes a real synthetic-story journey in which revised prose was carried into a subsequent continuation. Later records demonstrate native scene images, anticipatory artwork withheld until fresh selection, image reuse and a character portrait. Its Kaelen-originated proposal and Atelier relationship are distinct from its reuse of selected RP and Companion components. These application results belong to Infinite Manuscript, not automatically to the entire RP Host family.

**Maturity:** RP Host has documented Story-specific design and continuity components; the separately named Infinite Manuscript has a documented usable local Story alpha and later visual demonstrations. Its expanded goal remains open, including a physical-state narration finding and a pending browser regression. Neither establishes whole-novel fidelity, measured literary improvement, or a production release integrating all three pillars.

## Companion: continuity with an identity of its own

Companion work extends beyond narrative continuity. It includes a conversational builder, plain-language review of the resulting definition, packaged companion definitions, earlier live chat, recall, unfinished matters, and separate rehearsal and ongoing-contact lifecycles.

The product direction distinguishes creating a companion before first contact from interacting with that companion afterward. Preferences, disagreement and silence are not simply defects to edit away. A companion may participate in roleplay without their identity or shared history becoming interchangeable with the role being played.

Historical work includes prompted recall during live interaction and later isolated continuity-transition and recovery components. Those are different accomplishments: a structurally valid update does not prove its interpretation is correct, and a saved demonstration is not a new live exchange.

**Maturity:** substantial builder, client and continuity implementation, with bounded historical evaluations. The reviewed records do not establish one integrated, currently deployed companion release or reliable long-term recall across ordinary use. Affect, growth and portability work have their own limited evidence; they are not blanket claims of emotional accuracy, autonomous off-screen life or universal migration support.

## What connects the three

The common ambition is **continuity that preserves meaning, not just more conversation history**. The project asks who experienced an event, what it changed, what remains unsettled, and which later uses are justified. The answers differ between roleplay, authored stories and companionship; sharing engineering foundations does not erase those differences.

That is where the project's substance lies: connecting creative interaction to persistent state while preserving the distinctions that make the interaction worth continuing.

## Evidence, status and availability

The family contains implemented components and bounded historical demonstrations, not one production release integrating all three experiences. Each milestone belongs to its own project and version; ordinary-user return workflows, sustained recall and whole-book continuity remain open.
